## 0.1.0

* Initial release.
* `ContribGrid` widget renders a calendar-aligned heatmap from a `Map<DateTime, int>`.
* `ContribColorTheme` with `github` (light) and `githubDark` presets.
* Configurable date range, first day of week, intensity thresholds, cell size/spacing/radius.
* Optional month and weekday axis labels, tooltips, and `onCellTap` callback.
